# Contributing to Hanzo

Thank you for helping build Hanzo. This guide applies to every repository in the `hanzoai` and `hanzo-docs` organizations that does not have a `CONTRIBUTING.md` of its own. Full contributor onboarding lives at [docs.hanzo.ai/docs/contributing](https://docs.hanzo.ai/docs/contributing).

## Community & Discussions

Join the Hanzo community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XthHQQj). All contributor onboarding, engineering discussion, HIP proposals (`#hips`), and documentation feedback (`#docs`) happen on Discord rather than GitHub Discussions so maintainers and contributors stay in one place.

## Ways to contribute

- **Report a bug.** Open an issue with the steps to reproduce it, what you expected, and what happened.
- **Fix a bug or add a feature.** Pick an open issue on [hanzo.ai/bounty](https://hanzo.ai/bounty) or in any public `hanzoai` repository. For anything larger than a small fix, open an issue or ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XthHQQj) first so a maintainer can confirm the direction before you spend your time.
- **Improve the docs.** Drop broken links or missing steps in `#docs` on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XthHQQj) or open a pull request on [`hanzo-docs/docs`](https://github.com/hanzo-docs/docs).
- **Propose a HIP.** Submit architectural and protocol proposals in [`hanzoai/hips`](https://github.com/hanzoai/hips) ([hips.hanzo.ai](https://hips.hanzo.ai)).
- **Review.** Read an open pull request and share what you found.

## Pull requests

1. Fork the repository and branch from `main`. Keep the change small and focused.
2. Run the repository's tests and linters before you push; its `README.md` or `LLM.md` states the exact commands.
3. Say what changed and why, and link the issue.
4. A maintainer reviews it and either asks for changes or merges it.

## Security

Never report a vulnerability in a public issue or on Discord. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (`security@hanzo.ai`).

## Rewards

Merged contributions can earn points on Hanzo Rewards: what counts and what each earns is listed at https://hanzo.ai/bounty, and the board is at https://hanzo.ai/leaderboard. Maintainers decide what is awarded. Low-effort or automated submissions earn nothing and may be closed. If you endorse Hanzo publicly while taking part, say that you earn rewards for taking part.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
