# Code of Conduct

Hanzo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of the hanzoai organization.

In short: be respectful and patient, give and take feedback gracefully, assume good faith, and keep the work the point. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern, message an owner of the hanzoai organization on GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, and may restrict participation, in line with the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
